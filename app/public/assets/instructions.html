--- The rules ---
1) No previews!
2) No AI!
3) Stay in this editor at all times.
4) No measurement tools.
5) Stop coding when the time's up.
6) After the round is over, press "Finish" and follow the prompt instructions to see your results.

--- Instructions ---
1) Once started you can start typing in the editor.
2) Press the "assets" buton to open the assets panel, click an asset to copy its path to the clipboard.
3) Click the reference image thumbnail to open a larger version of the reference image.
4) Press the "instructions" button to read these instructions at any time.

Good luck and most important of all; have fun!

--- Tips ---
1) Browsers set default styling on a lot of elements.
2) Without a doctype and a box-sizing property, you enter quirks mode land.
3) The SVG assets have inline sizes, but can you trust them?
